For a simply supported beam with a point load P placed a metres from the left support, Euler–Bernoulli beam theory gives the deflection under that load as δa = Pa²(L−a)²/(3EIL). The drawn curve uses the corresponding piecewise equation along the whole span, not a fixed bow shape. For a rectangular section, I = bh³/12. The material is representative No. 2 spruce–pine–fir: E = 8.27 GPa and reference bending value Fb = 6.03 MPa (1.2×106 and 875 psi). The red region begins where σ = Mc/I exceeds Fb; this is a conservative design threshold, not a prediction of the exact crack load. Real lumber strength varies with grade, knots, moisture, load duration, size, and restraint, and design requires the applicable adjustment factors. The L/360 line is a serviceability comparison for visible sag. Sag is shown at a fixed 12× magnification, capped after failure so extreme elastic predictions do not run off the diagram.
