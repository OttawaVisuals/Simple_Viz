## 1. Concept

- **Equation(s):** Equation of Hagen–Poiseuille - horizontal
- **Real numbers / reference values to use:** Taskmaster episode where Tim Vine tries to blow a Candle using standard plastic straws assembled end-to-end but it doesn't work (length of maybe 1.5m) but manages to do that with a garden hose (maybe 10m long)
- **The one insight the page should deliver:** after playing with this, the user should feel that they understand why sucking different liquid through different length of straws is harder/easier.

## 2. Page layout

- **Reading order** (numbered, top to bottom):
  1. Witty title + simple subtitle about straws and sucking/blowing
  2. Simple and elegant SVG icon across the width of the page that shows someone drinking through a long straw
  3. Grouping of selectors:
    3.1. (left) Fluid: 4 buttons (Air, Water, Milkshake, Honey) on the right side of Drop icon. Shows the fluid viscosity selected. 
    3.2. (middle) Length: slider from .1m to 10m on the right side of the Length icon. Shows the length selected (m).
    3.3. (right) Diameter: slider from 1.5mm to 10mm on the right side of the Diamter icon. Shows the diamter selected (mm).
  4. Equation in scientific format (i.e. the denominator is under the line). Text should be big (main part of the page) and in the middle of the page. Have simple arrows linking the elements of the equation to the selectors above.
  5. Large horizontal bar chart with green | orange | red sections (based on the human possibilities for the selected fluid) and position of the deltaP on it.


- **Grouping**: Connection between the equation and the selectors with arrows.
- **Dominant element:** Equation and item 5.
