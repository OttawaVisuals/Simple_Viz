How long to almost reach light speed?

Hold a rocket at a steady felt acceleration and watch the Solar System shrink behind it. At 1 g, one year aboard reaches 76% of c; in Earth’s frame, the rocket has travelled about half a light-year from its starting point.

A massive rocket never reaches light speed. Constant proper acceleration is the acceleration passengers feel. Starting from rest, v = c tanh(ατ/c), Earth-frame distance x = c²[cosh(ατ/c) − 1]/α, and kinetic energy Ek = (γ − 1)mc². The star directions are schematic; radial distances are to scale. Energy is for the selected dry mass only and excludes propellant, exhaust, inefficiency and braking. Sources: Wikipedia on hyperbolic motion, relativistic kinetic energy, the heliosphere, Oort Cloud, Alpha Centauri and Voyager 1.
