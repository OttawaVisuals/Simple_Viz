Distances come from NASA/JPL Horizons geometric Earth-centred vectors sampled daily from 2000 through 2050. The selected date is the moment the light arrives at Earth; the page calculates when it left the object. Shortest and longest dates were refined hourly; average is the arithmetic mean of the daily distances, represented by the nearest sampled date. “Relative position changed” compares the Earth-to-object vector at those two moments — it is not the object's path length. The upward movement uses JPL mean diameters; sub-pixel changes are enlarged and very large changes are compressed, with either adjustment labelled. Download the 149,024-row dataset or view the calculated summary. Horizontal distances are compressed.
